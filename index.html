how it works

Pick four things that make up your creative practice — writing, engaging, sharing, reaching new readers, or whatever fits your own Substack or blog.

Give each one a name, a unit, and a weekly target. The defaults are 100 each, so a full week is four hundred — but every number here is yours to change.

Tap +1, the big step button, or edit a goal with the pencil to rename it, change its unit, or set a new target and step.

Your marks are saved on this device only. Use keep a copy to view, save, or copy your week as text.
